#Team Charter :#
Utrecht Legends - Group 4
Members: Adityanand, Faezeh, Sepideh

###Potential Areas of Agreement for our team:###

1.	Work Hours – Team members may start at their own convenient and work daily; core collaboration hours are 11 am – 5 pm every Tuesdays, Thursday (except Dutch class) and Friday. 
For Monday & Wednesday: We have class and may stay after school for work
2.	Daily Commitment – Everyone aims for at least 5 hours of focused work per day, barring illness or emergencies.
3.	Stand-ups – We hold a daily Scrum at 11 a.m., everyone attends on time. If we have class or may be meeting at school then joining standup is not necessary.
4.	Sprint Reviews & Retros – Attendance at sprint review and retrospective is mandatory, unless there’s a serious conflict. This occurs every biweekly by 4pm
5.	Communication Tools – WhatsApp/Teams for quick chats, Excel/GitLab for tasks, email only for external communication.
6.	Transparency – Update members daily so progress is visible to all.
7.	Deliverables – When we commit in sprint planning, we own the delivery unless new impediments arise.
8.	Asking for Help – If blocked for more than 1 hour, raise it in chat or during stand-up.
9.	Respecting Meetings – Come prepared, keep cameras on (if remote), and keep meetings on-topic. 
10.	Definition of Done – A story isn’t done until it meets our agreed DoD (tested, documented, integrated, reviewed).
11.	Code & Quality Standards – Follow agreed coding conventions, peer reviews. Our team follows a set of lightweight coding standards to ensure clarity, maintainability, and loose coupling in our system. Variables and methods use camelCase with clear, descriptive names that reflect intent, while classes are named in PascalCase and follow the single-responsibility principle, with each file containing only one class. Booleans are expressed as conditions (e.g., isAvailable, hasCargo) to improve readability.
12.	Late Arrivals – If you’ll be late to a meeting, send a quick note to the group. No silent absences.
13.	Missed Commitments – If you can’t deliver something you promised, raise it early so we can adapt.
14.	Respect & Inclusion – We value different perspectives; all voices are heard in discussions. 
15.	Celebrating Wins – Acknowledge progress and celebrate achievements, big or small.




Handling Situations
•	If someone is late: They notify the team chat; if missed, they catch up with the meeting notes/recording. No judgment, just accountability.
•	If communication breaks down: Escalate to Scrum Master, who helps resolve the conflict and restore flow.
